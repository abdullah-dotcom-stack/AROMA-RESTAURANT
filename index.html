Egyptian cooking, refined.

AROMA-RESTAURANT opened in 2012 with a simple idea: take the dishes Egyptian families have cooked for generations and serve them with the care of a fine-dining kitchen.

Our lamb is braised for six hours, our fish arrives every morning, and our bread comes out of the oven minutes before it reaches your table. Nothing is reheated. Nothing is rushed.
